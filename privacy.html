Overview

ChemicalResistance.org provides chemical resistance reference data. We respect your privacy and are committed to keeping your experience safe and transparent.

Information We Collect

We do not require you to create an account or provide personal information. We do not collect names, email addresses, or other personally identifying information through the site.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use our site. This service may collect anonymous usage data including pages visited, time spent, browser type, and approximate location. This data is aggregated and cannot identify individual users.

Cookies

Our analytics service uses cookies to distinguish unique visitors. These cookies do not contain personal information.

Third-Party Services

Our site is hosted on GitHub Pages and uses Cloudflare for content delivery and security. These services may collect standard server logs.

Google Fonts: Our site loads the Inter font from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com). This sends your IP address to Google's servers. See Google's Privacy Policy for details.

Lucide Icons (unpkg.com): Our site loads the Lucide icon library via the unpkg.com CDN. This may log your IP address. See unpkg privacy information for details.
