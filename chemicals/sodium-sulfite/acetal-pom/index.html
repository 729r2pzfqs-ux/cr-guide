FAQ

Is Acetal (POM) resistant to Sodium Sulfite?

Acetal (POM) has Excellent (A) resistance to Sodium Sulfite at 20°C. Excellent resistance - recommended for continuous use.

Can I store Sodium Sulfite in Acetal (POM) containers?

Yes, Acetal (POM) is rated A (Excellent) for Sodium Sulfite.
