Carbon Tetrachloride Resistance of HDPE

Is HDPE compatible with Carbon Tetrachloride? See the chemical resistance rating below.

Compatibility Rating

At 20°C (68°F)
D
Poor
Not recommended - significant degradation expected
At 50°C (122°F)
D
Poor
Not recommended - significant degradation expected

Why is HDPE rated this way to Carbon Tetrachloride?

HDPE is not recommended for use with Carbon Tetrachloride. Significant degradation or failure is expected.

⚠️ When NOT to use HDPE

Avoid using High-Density Polyethylene with strong oxidizing acids (nitric, chromic), chlorinated solvents, and aromatic hydrocarbons. Maximum service temperature is around 80°C.
