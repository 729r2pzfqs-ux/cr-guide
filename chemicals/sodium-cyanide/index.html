Frequently Asked Questions

Which materials resist Sodium Cyanide at high concentrations?

The materials with an A rating (resistant) for Sodium Cyanide at 20 °C are: PTFE, HDPE, PP, PVDF, EPDM, Viton (FPM), NBR, LDPE. These can generally be used in direct contact.

Which materials should be avoided with Sodium Cyanide?

Materials rated D (not resistant) for Sodium Cyanide are: Aluminium. These should not be used in contact with Sodium Cyanide.

Does temperature affect chemical resistance to Sodium Cyanide?

For Sodium Cyanide, all tested materials maintain the same rating at both 20 °C and 50 °C.

Is PTFE safe for Sodium Cyanide?

PTFE is rated A for Sodium Cyanide at 20 °C. A rating of A means resistant, B means limited resistance, C means partly resistant, and D means not resistant.

Does concentration affect chemical resistance to Sodium Cyanide?

Yes. Sodium Cyanide is tested at 2 concentrations (aqueous, saturated). Some materials change their rating depending on the concentration.

About this data

The ratings come from the chemical resistance list published by Bürkle GmbH, which compiled them from raw-material manufacturers’ data. They describe laboratory tests on the raw material at 20 °C and 50 °C.

A finished part also sees pressure, mechanical stress, welds and mixtures that a laboratory test does not. Treat the rating as a first filter and test under your own conditions before relying on it.
