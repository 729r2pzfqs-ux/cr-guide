已更正和存在争议的数值

  • 304不锈钢（20 %）：原始值 2/3，显示为 4/4。 The British Stainless Steel Association limits 304 to about 5 % sulfuric acid at room temperature; intermediate concentrations corrode it rapidly. The source rates it B. [1] [2]
  • 304不锈钢（40 %）：原始值 2/3，显示为 4/4。 Neither 304 nor 316 resists sulfuric acid of intermediate concentration: the British Stainless Steel Association limits 304 to about 5 % and 316 to about 22 % at room temperature. The source rates both B. [1] [2]
  • 316不锈钢（40 %）：原始值 2/3，显示为 4/4。 Neither 304 nor 316 resists sulfuric acid of intermediate concentration: the British Stainless Steel Association limits 304 to about 5 % and 316 to about 22 % at room temperature. The source rates both B. [1] [2]
  • 304不锈钢（80 %）：原始值 2/4，显示为 4/4。 Neither 304 nor 316 resists sulfuric acid of intermediate concentration: the British Stainless Steel Association limits 304 to about 5 % and 316 to about 22 % at room temperature. The source rates both B. [1] [2]
  • 316不锈钢（80 %）：原始值 2/3，显示为 4/4。 Neither 304 nor 316 resists sulfuric acid of intermediate concentration: the British Stainless Steel Association limits 304 to about 5 % and 316 to about 22 % at room temperature. The source rates both B. [1] [2]

原始数值采用 1（很好）至 4（不耐受）的标度；1 = A，4 = D。

关于数据

等级数据来自 Bürkle GmbH 发布的耐化学性列表，该列表根据原材料制造商的数据汇编而成，反映原材料在 20 °C 和 50 °C 下的实验室测试结果。

成品部件还会受到压力、机械应力、焊缝和混合物的影响，这些是实验室测试无法涵盖的。请将等级作为初步筛选依据，并在实际工况下测试后再采用。
