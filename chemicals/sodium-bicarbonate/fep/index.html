FAQ

Is FEP resistant to Sodium Bicarbonate?

FEP has Excellent (A) resistance to Sodium Bicarbonate at 20°C. Excellent resistance - recommended for continuous use.

Can I store Sodium Bicarbonate in FEP containers?

Based on the A rating, FEP is suitable for Sodium Bicarbonate storage. At 50°C, the rating remains stable.
