Chlorine Water Resistance of Polypropylene (PP)

Is Polypropylene (PP) compatible with Chlorine Water? See the chemical resistance rating below.

Compatibility Rating

At 20°C (68°F)
C
Fair
Limited resistance - short-term exposure only
At 50°C (122°F)
D
Poor
Not recommended - significant degradation expected

Why is PP rated this way to Chlorine Water?

PP has limited resistance to Chlorine Water. Short-term exposure may be acceptable, but prolonged contact is not recommended.

⚠️ When NOT to use PP

Avoid using Polypropylene with concentrated oxidizing acids, chlorinated solvents, and aromatic hydrocarbons. Becomes brittle below 0°C.
