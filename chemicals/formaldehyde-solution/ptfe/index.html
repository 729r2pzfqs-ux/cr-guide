FAQ

Is PTFE resistant to Formaldehyde Solution?

PTFE has Excellent (A) resistance to Formaldehyde Solution at 20°C. Excellent resistance - recommended for continuous use.

Can I store Formaldehyde Solution in PTFE containers?

Based on the A rating, PTFE is suitable for Formaldehyde Solution storage. At 50°C, the rating remains stable.
