Frequently Asked Questions

Is ECTFE/ETFE resistant to Diesel Fuel?

ECTFE/ETFE has No Data (NR) resistance to Diesel Fuel at 20°C. No data available.

Can I store Diesel Fuel in ECTFE/ETFE containers?

Based on the NR rating at room temperature, ECTFE/ETFE may not be ideal for Diesel Fuel storage. At elevated temperatures (50°C), the rating remains stable. Always verify with your container supplier.

What affects chemical resistance?

Temperature, concentration, exposure duration, and mechanical stress all affect compatibility. Our ratings assume standard conditions - always test for critical applications.
