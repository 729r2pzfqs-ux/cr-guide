The two materials

PVC Flexible

Flexible (plasticised) polyvinyl chloride

Flexible PVC shares the solvent weaknesses of rigid PVC, and any liquid that extracts the plasticiser also hardens it. It is generally less resistant than rigid PVC.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is PVC Flexible or Polystyrene more chemical-resistant?

Of 274 chemicals rated for both materials at 20 °C, PVC Flexible and Polystyrene share the same rating in 204 rows (74 %). PVC Flexible is rated better in 32 rows and Polystyrene in 38 rows.

How do PVC Flexible and Polystyrene compare for chemical resistance?

Polystyrene is rated better with Alcohols and glycols, Amines and nitrogen compounds, Oxidising agents and halogens, Organic acids. They perform similarly with Mineral acids, Alkalis, Salts and salt solutions, Ketones and aldehydes.

About this data

The ratings come from the chemical resistance list published by Bürkle GmbH, which compiled them from raw-material manufacturers’ data. They describe laboratory tests on the raw material at 20 °C and 50 °C.

A finished part also sees pressure, mechanical stress, welds and mixtures that a laboratory test does not. Treat the rating as a first filter and test under your own conditions before relying on it.
