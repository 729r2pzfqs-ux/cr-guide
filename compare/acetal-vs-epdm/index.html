Frequently Asked Questions

Is Acetal (POM) or EPDM more chemical-resistant?

Of 954 chemicals rated for both materials at 20 °C, Acetal (POM) and EPDM share the same rating in 321 rows (34 %). Acetal (POM) is rated better in 365 rows and EPDM in 268 rows.

How do Acetal (POM) and EPDM compare for chemical resistance?

Based on 954 chemicals tested for both: Acetal (POM) is rated better with Aromatic hydrocarbons, Vegetable and animal oils, fats, essential oils, Fuels, mineral oils and aliphatic hydrocarbons, Esters and ethers. EPDM is rated better with Mineral acids, Alkalis, Salts and salt solutions, Oxidising agents and halogens. They perform similarly with Phenols, Foods and beverages.

About this data

The ratings come from the chemical resistance list published by Bürkle GmbH, which compiled them from raw-material manufacturers’ data. They describe laboratory tests on the raw material at 20 °C and 50 °C.

A finished part also sees pressure, mechanical stress, welds and mixtures that a laboratory test does not. Treat the rating as a first filter and test under your own conditions before relying on it.
