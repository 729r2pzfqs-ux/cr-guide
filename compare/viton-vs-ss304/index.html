Frequently Asked Questions

Is Viton (FPM) or SS 304 more chemical-resistant?

Of 854 chemicals rated for both materials at 20 °C, Viton (FPM) and SS 304 share the same rating in 446 rows (52 %). Viton (FPM) is rated better in 89 rows and SS 304 in 319 rows.

How do Viton (FPM) and SS 304 compare for chemical resistance?

Based on 854 chemicals tested for both: Viton (FPM) is rated better with Mineral acids, Oxidising agents and halogens. SS 304 is rated better with Ketones and aldehydes, Esters and ethers, Phenols, Amines and nitrogen compounds. They perform similarly with Salts and salt solutions, Fuels, mineral oils and aliphatic hydrocarbons, Foods and beverages.

About this data

The ratings come from the chemical resistance list published by Bürkle GmbH, which compiled them from raw-material manufacturers’ data. They describe laboratory tests on the raw material at 20 °C and 50 °C.

A finished part also sees pressure, mechanical stress, welds and mixtures that a laboratory test does not. Treat the rating as a first filter and test under your own conditions before relying on it.
