Frequently Asked Questions

Is PMP or NBR more chemical-resistant?

Of 416 chemicals rated for both materials at 20 °C, PMP and NBR share the same rating in 210 rows (50 %). PMP is rated better in 175 rows and NBR in 31 rows.

How do PMP and NBR compare for chemical resistance?

Based on 416 chemicals tested for both: PMP is rated better with Mineral acids, Oxidising agents and halogens, Alcohols and glycols, Organic acids. NBR is rated better with Vegetable and animal oils, fats, essential oils, Fuels, mineral oils and aliphatic hydrocarbons, Halogenated hydrocarbons. They perform similarly with Aromatic hydrocarbons, Foods and beverages.

About this data

The ratings come from the chemical resistance list published by Bürkle GmbH, which compiled them from raw-material manufacturers’ data. They describe laboratory tests on the raw material at 20 °C and 50 °C.

A finished part also sees pressure, mechanical stress, welds and mixtures that a laboratory test does not. Treat the rating as a first filter and test under your own conditions before relying on it.
