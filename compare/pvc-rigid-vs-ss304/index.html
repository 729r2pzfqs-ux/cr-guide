The two materials

PVC Rigid

Rigid (unplasticised) polyvinyl chloride

Rigid PVC resists acids, alkalis and salt solutions well. Ketones, esters, aromatic and chlorinated solvents dissolve or swell it. Service temperature is limited to about 60 °C.

Rated rows: 569. A 62 %, B 8 %, C 9 %, D 21 %

SS 304

Stainless steel 1.4301 (AISI 304)

Stainless steel 304 (1.4301) resists oxidising acids and most organic chemicals. Chlorides cause pitting and stress-corrosion cracking.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is PVC Rigid or SS 304 more chemical-resistant?

Of 516 chemicals rated for both materials at 20 °C, PVC Rigid and SS 304 share the same rating in 280 rows (54 %). PVC Rigid is rated better in 64 rows and SS 304 in 172 rows.

How do PVC Rigid and SS 304 compare for chemical resistance?

Based on 516 chemicals tested for both: PVC Rigid is rated better with Oxidising agents and halogens, Mineral acids. SS 304 is rated better with Aromatic hydrocarbons, Esters and ethers, Ketones and aldehydes, Halogenated hydrocarbons. They perform similarly with Organic acids, Alkalis, Salts and salt solutions, Foods and beverages.

About this data

The ratings come from the chemical resistance list published by Bürkle GmbH, which compiled them from raw-material manufacturers’ data. They describe laboratory tests on the raw material at 20 °C and 50 °C.

A finished part also sees pressure, mechanical stress, welds and mixtures that a laboratory test does not. Treat the rating as a first filter and test under your own conditions before relying on it.
