ETFE vs ECTFE

Side-by-side chemical resistance comparison to help you choose the right material.

ETFE

Ethylene Tetrafluoroethylene

ETFE offers excellent chemical resistance with good mechanical strength. It's transparent and recyclable, commonly used for architectural glazing and chemical processing.

Temperature Range -100°C to 150°C
Excellent (A) Ratings 352 chemicals
Compatible (A+B) 373 chemicals
View full ETFE chart →

ECTFE

Ethylene Chlorotrifluoroethylene

ECTFE has superior chemical resistance, especially to chlorinated solvents. It offers better permeation resistance and is preferred for aggressive chemical environments.

Temperature Range -76°C to 150°C
Excellent (A) Ratings 352 chemicals
Compatible (A+B) 373 chemicals
View full ECTFE chart →

When to Use Each Material

Choose ETFE when:

Architectural applications, wire insulation, solar panel covers, general chemical processing where transparency is valued.

Choose ECTFE when:

Aggressive chemical environments, chlorinated solvent handling, tank linings, exhaust systems with acid fumes.
