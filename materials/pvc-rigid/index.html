Chemical storage tanks for mild chemicals

Laboratory fume extraction ductwork

Not Recommended For

PVC Rigid may degrade when exposed to:

  • Ketones and esters
  • Chlorinated solvents
  • Aromatic hydrocarbons
  • Temperatures above 60°C

Tip: PVC has a low temperature limit. For higher temperatures, use PP, HDPE, or PVDF.
