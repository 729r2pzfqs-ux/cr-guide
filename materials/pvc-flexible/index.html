PVC Flexible Properties

Full Name Plasticized PVC (pPVC)
Max Temperature 60°C (140°F)
Key Property Flexible, economical
Plasticizer DOP/DEHP or phthalate-free

Common Applications

Tubing

Laboratory and chemical transfer tubing

Hoses

Air and water hoses, suction hoses

Liners

Tank liners and pond liners

Not Recommended For

PVC Flexible may degrade when exposed to:

  • Ketones and esters
  • Chlorinated solvents
  • Most organic solvents
  • Concentrated acids

Tip: Plasticizer can leach into solvents. For pure chemical contact, use rigid materials like PP or PTFE.
