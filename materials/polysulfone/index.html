Polysulfone Properties

Full Name Polysulfone (PSU)
Max Temperature 160°C (320°F)
Min Temperature -100°C (-148°F)
Key Property Steam sterilizable, transparent

Common Applications

Medical Devices

Dialysis membranes, surgical instruments

Water Filtration

Ultrafiltration and nanofiltration membranes

Hot Water

Hot water fittings, coffee machine components

Not Recommended For

Polysulfone may degrade when exposed to:

  • Ketones (acetone, MEK)
  • Chlorinated solvents
  • Aromatic hydrocarbons
  • Strong alkalis at high concentration

Tip: For solvent resistance, consider PVDF. For universal chemical resistance, use PTFE.
