SAN Properties

Full Name Styrene Acrylonitrile (SAN)
Max Temperature 85°C (185°F)
Transparency Clear
Key Property Better resistance than PS

Common Applications

Lab Equipment

Graduated cylinders, beakers, cuvettes

Food Processing

Clear containers for food contact

Housings

Instrument housings and covers

Not Recommended For

SAN may degrade when exposed to:

  • Most organic solvents
  • Acetone and MEK
  • Strong acids at elevated temperatures
  • Chlorinated solvents

Tip: For better chemical resistance with clarity, consider PETG or PMP.
