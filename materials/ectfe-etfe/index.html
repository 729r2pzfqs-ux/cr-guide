Common Applications

Chemical tank and vessel linings for corrosive environments

Architecture

ETFE roofing and cladding panels

Wire Insulation

High-performance electrical insulation

Not Recommended For

ECTFE/ETFE may degrade when exposed to:

  • Molten alkali metals
  • Fluorine gas at high temperature
  • Some amine-based solvents at elevated temperatures

Tip: For the most extreme chemical environments, consider PTFE which has broader resistance.
