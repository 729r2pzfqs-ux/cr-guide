Polystyrene Properties

Full Name Polystyrene (PS)
Max Temperature 70°C (158°F)
Transparency Clear (GPPS)
Key Property Very low cost

Common Applications

Disposable Lab

Petri dishes, cuvettes, pipette tips

Packaging

Chemical sample containers

Insulation

EPS foam insulation for cold chain

Not Recommended For

Polystyrene may degrade when exposed to:

  • Most organic solvents
  • Acetone, MEK, toluene
  • Essential oils
  • Concentrated acids

Tip: PS has poor solvent resistance. For reusable chemical equipment, use PP, HDPE, or PETG.
