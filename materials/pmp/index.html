PMP Properties

Full Name Polymethylpentene (TPX®)
Max Temperature 170°C (338°F)
Density 0.83 g/cm³ (lightest plastic)
Autoclavable Yes (121°C)

Common Applications

Lab Equipment

Beakers, graduated cylinders, funnels

Microwave

Microwave-transparent containers

Medical

Sterilizable medical components

Not Recommended For

PMP may degrade when exposed to:

  • Chlorinated solvents
  • Strong oxidizing acids (>60% nitric)
  • Aromatic hydrocarbons at high temperature

Tip: PMP is brittle compared to PC or PETG. For impact resistance, choose polycarbonate.
