聚氨酯 Chemical Resistance Chart

聚氨酯性能

Full Name Polyurethane (PU/PUR)
Max Temperature 80–90 °C (176–194 °F)
Shore Hardness 20A–75D
Min Temperature −40 °C (−40 °F)
Key Property Abrasion resistance, load bearing
Density 1.05–1.25 g/cm³

常见应用

Industrial Rollers

Conveyor rollers, printing rollers, drive wheels

Seals & Gaskets

Hydraulic seals, O-rings, wear rings, scrapers

Coatings & Linings

Tank linings, pipe coatings, floor coatings

不推荐用于

聚氨酯 may degrade when exposed to:

  • Ketones (acetone, MEK)
  • Chlorinated solvents (TCE, carbon tet)
  • Aromatic solvents (benzene, toluene, xylene)
  • Strong acids (concentrated HCl, HNO₃)
  • Esters (ethyl acetate)
  • Hot water above 80 °C (long-term)

Tip: For ketones and esters, use PTFE or FEP. For aggressive acids at high temperatures, use PVDF. For oil-based applications, PU outperforms EPDM and silicone.
